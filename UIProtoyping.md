**Heuristic Evaluation**

-Visibility of system status

Our UI aims to make the system status obvious to users through clear titles and messaging. Each page of our UI has a title at the top indicating to the user what they are accessing at the moment, such as “Welcome to CommonGround” for the account creation page, “Your Profile” for the user’s profile page, and “Friends” for the user’s friends page. We also use intuitive markers to indicate status. For example, completed missions are signaled with checkmarks and nearby friends have a green dot with the words “exploring nearby” to indicate nearness.

-Match system words to the real world

Our UI uses common terminology for customer accessibility. No coding/programmer jargon is ever displayed to the user to limit confusion. For example, users can tap the “edit” button to change their profile.

-User control and freedom

Our UI allows for user control and freedom through system autonomy and easy editing. For onboarding, although our UI has a clear pipeline of steps, users can revert to the previous page at any time by simply clicking the back button for whatever mobile browser they are using at the moment. Past login, users have free access across their home, friends, and profile pages. Users can also undo important actions easily; for instance, in their profile page, they can easily toggle and input their preferences for map visibility and range discovery.

-Consistency and standards

Our UI maintains a consistent language, aesthetic, and usability across the entire experience. Our onboarding process mimics the standard of many other software logins, making the process straightforward to complete. Moreover, our font and language remain consistent throughout the software, using real-world language so users know what to expect when they click specific buttons. Particularly for our font, we use the same, readable font across the entire product, with bigger and smaller fonts applied according to the importance of specific titles/actions/etc.

-Error prevention

Our UI supports error prevention with clear labelling, warning information for users, and easy reediting for saved mistakes. All our pages have clear labelling of titles, buttons, and more which reduces errors from misunderstanding of use. We include understandable warnings/information for users to increase their awareness regarding arguably the most important aspect of the app: their privacy. For instance, the onboarding page where users can set their radius includes a disclaimer regarding their location sharing. Moreover, should users ever change their mind about their profile settings or mistakenly input the wrong settings, they can easily go back and change it, where it will be automatically saved.

-Recognition rather than recall

Our UI reduces the amount of information users need to memorize by keeping important actions and navigation visible throughout the app. A consistent bottom navigation bar is displayed across the Home, Friends, and Profile pages so users can quickly recognize where they are and move between sections without remembering extra steps. During onboarding, selected interests are highlighted with darker green tiles and icons, making it easy for users to recognize their current choices instead of memorizing them manually. On the friend page, shared interest tags such as “gaming”, “hiking”, or “cooking” are directly displayed under each user profile to help users immediately recognize common interests. Nearby users are also labeled with indicators like “exploring nearby,” which helps users quickly understand status information without confusion.

-Accelerators

Our app includes several features that help returning users navigate and complete actions more effectively. The persistent navigation bar allows users to quickly switch between major sections of the app with a single tap instead of returning through multiple screens. On the Profile page, users are able to directly edit their bio, interests, discovery range, and visibility settings from one centralized location, reducing unnecessary navigation steps. Saved chat and friend connections also allow users to continue a conversation without needing to rematch with users every time they reopen the app. In addition, the Mission section encourages faster engagement by giving users quick access to goals and progress tracking directly from the Home screen.

-Minimalist design

Our UI follows a clean and minimalist design style using a consistent green color palette, simple icons, rounded cards, and organized spacing to avoid overwhelming users. Each screen focuses only on the most important information and interactions needed for that page. For example, the onboarding screens guide users through one step at a time, such as selecting interests or setting a discovery radius, instead of presenting all settings at once. The map screen mainly focuses on nearby users, missions, and navigation controls while avoiding unnecessary clutter. Similarly, the Friends page only displays essential information such as profile icons, shared interests, and nearby status labels, helping users scan information quickly and easily.

-Help users recognize and recover from errors

Our UI provides clear feedback when users make mistakes or attempt invalid actions so they can easily recover from errors. During onboarding, users must select at least one interest and can select a maximum of ten interests. If a user tries to exceed the limit, the system displays a warning banner explaining the issue and instructing the user to deselect another interest before continuing. Required profile fields are also validated before saving, and the system notifies users if information is missing or invalid. In chat-related interactions, failed chat requests or connection problems would display simple error messages instead of technical language so users can understand what happened and retry the action if needed. These messages help users understand the issue and continue using the app more easily.

-Help and documentation

The onboarding process helps guide users through the app step-by-step before they begin using the main features. Each onboarding screen includes short instructions, labels, and visual feedback to help users understand how interest selection, matching, and discovery settings work. Features such as “Visible on Map” and “Discovery Range” are clearly labeled so users can easily understand privacy-related settings without needing additional explanations. Visual indicators like highlighted interests, mission checkmarks, and nearby status labels also help guide users while using the app. In the future, the app could include a dedicated Help or FAQ page for users who need additional support with matching, missions, or privacy settings.
